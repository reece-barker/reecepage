## The work

I spent three summers in the IT department at Munich International School, from 2021 to 2023, the last one covering
IT and finance. From June 2024 to July 2026 I was back on contract, supporting IT and building apps and automations
on Microsoft's Power Platform that take repetitive admin work off people's desks.

## Purchase orders, before and after

**Before,** a purchase order was a Microsoft Form. Power Automate copied each answer into Excel sheets and sent the
approvals round by email.

**After,** I rebuilt it in Power Apps, all on the Power Platform. The finance team sees the data in a clearer view, the totals are calculated automatically, and amounts in other currencies are converted with exchange rates
fetched automatically from a public source.
