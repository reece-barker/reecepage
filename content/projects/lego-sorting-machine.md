## Three ways to sort

The machine sorts in three different ways:

1. **By colour,** with a colour sensor.
2. **By size,** with a mechanical shaker that drops each part through the right hole.
3. **By shape,** with a camera and an open source machine learning model that recognises the specific part.

## My part

My role in this team is software advisor. I gave the team pointers on the software side, the build and the
recognition itself are their work.
