## The idea

Every room has a climate: temperature, humidity, air quality and light. Most buildings already have systems that
control each of these.

Our software connects to the systems a building already has and controls them together, adjusting to who is
actually there. We do not build our own heaters or air conditioners. The product is the control layer that sits
on top of the air conditioning, ventilation, radiators and lighting that are already installed.

## Where it came from

Cognitive Atmospherics grew out of AI-Powered Microclimate Guardian, a Saxion research project that was funded
through crowdfunding. We founded the company out of it in June 2026.

## My part

I look after operations, software and the business side: investors, the company's financing, and making sure
everyone in the team gets done what they need to do.

## Where it helps

- **At home:** comfort with less wasted energy.
- **In industry:** stable conditions for people, products and machines.
- **In healthcare,** where it matters most: a room tuned to each patient, to support better care and recovery.

The plan is to start with homes, then move on to schools and hospitals.

## Where it stands

The four of us have the legal groundwork and a twelve month roadmap in place. Saxion's Entrepreneurship Programme
awarded us €2,500, and we are now building our second prototype. Questions about the company go to
[hello@cognitiveatmospherics.com](mailto:hello@cognitiveatmospherics.com).
