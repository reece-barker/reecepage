## What it is

Online communities get questions, reports and requests all day. This system turns each one into a ticket inside
Discord, where the community already is, and connects to the external APIs these communities already rely on.

I built it during my freelance years and sold it commercially on two marketplaces, to almost ten customers. It
handles support in real time, with role based access and tickets sorted into categories automatically.
