## The brand

The mark is a dove. The palette is olive, sage, blush and cream, the type is Young Serif for headings and Nunito
Sans for everything else.

Around it I built small tools that keep everything consistent: builders for business cards, letters, invoices,
contracts, slides, social posts and app icons. They all work from the same colours, type and details, so nothing
has to be redrawn by hand.

## The site

The home page is a tree with three branches: code, earth and people. Every project on this site grows from one of
them and carries its colour. The leaves are small squares, because it is a computer science tree.

Under the surface it is a server rendered Express application. The [article on how it is built](/writing/how-this-site-is-built)
goes through the choices.
